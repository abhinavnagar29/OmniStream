Topic 3 (Combined Recommendation System)

Build a generalized recommendation platform that aggregates and suggests content across multiple domains such as videos, music, podcasts, movies, and news.

The system should overcome disconnected platforms by providing personalized recommendations from diverse content sources in a single interface.
It should understand user preferences across formats and deliver relevant, cross-domain suggestions.

The goal is to create a unified, scalable, and user-centric recommendation experience usable in real-world scenarios.
